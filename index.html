FAQ

Quick answers

What is the minimum order?

The minimum order is 50 pieces per design. The more boxes you order, the lower the price per box.

How much do the boxes cost?

Every box is custom, so the price depends on size, material, finishes and quantity. Send your details through Shop Now and we will reply with an exact price.

Can I get a sample first?

Yes, paid samples are available. The sample price depends on your box size, style and finishes. Use the "Order a Paid Sample" button and we will confirm the cost.

Can you print my logo?

Yes. Send your logo and we will add it with the finish you choose: foil, embossing, spot UV or print.

Which countries do you deliver to?

We deliver across the United Kingdom, the USA, Canada and Europe. Shipping is included in your quote.
