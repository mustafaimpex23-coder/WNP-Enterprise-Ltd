FAQ

Common questions

What is your minimum order quantity?

Our minimums are low so new brands can start small. The exact MOQ depends on the box style and finishes. Send us your details and we will confirm it in your quote.

How much do the boxes cost?

Every box is custom, so the price depends on size, material, finishes and quantity. Fill in the quote form and we will send an exact price within 24 hours.

Can I get a sample before the full order?

Yes, paid samples are available. The sample price depends on your box size, style and finishes. We share the exact sample cost with your quote.

Do you help with the design?

Yes. Send your logo and product size and we will help set up the box structure and artwork for production.

Which countries do you deliver to?

We deliver across the United Kingdom, the USA, Canada and Europe. Shipping cost and time are included in your quote.

How long does production take?

Timelines depend on quantity and finishes. We give you a clear production and delivery date before you confirm your order.
